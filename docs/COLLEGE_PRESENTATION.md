# College presentation checklist

1. Problem statement and motivation
2. Existing approach and proposed system
3. Dataset provenance, schema, and limitations
4. Cleaning and feature encoding
5. Train/test methodology
6. Baseline and candidate regressors
7. MAE, RMSE, and R-squared comparison
8. Actual-versus-predicted and residual plots
9. Feature importance (when supported by the selected estimator)
10. Flask demo, API, and deployment architecture
11. Limitations, ethical use, and future scope

Recommended viva questions: Why regression? Why hold out test data? What is data leakage? Explain MAE versus RMSE. Why can synthetic results not prove real-world accuracy? What happens when the model artifact is missing? How would you improve geographic and temporal generalization?
